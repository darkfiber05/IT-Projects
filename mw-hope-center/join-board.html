Executive Leadership Committee (ELC)

Our mission

  • We exist to introduce the MW Hope Center to the next generation of advocates, volunteers, and donors who will support the Hope Center's future success.
  • We seek to gain a better understanding of inequality and how it impacts people and communities.
  • We live with purpose and intentionality and challenge ourselves daily to be our best.
  • We publicly support the MW Hope Center's mission and leverage our resources (time, finances, and/or connections).

Member qualifications

If you are passionate about the Hope Center and are willing to invest your time and talents, we welcome you to apply.

  • Minimum 21 years of age
  • Align with MW Hope Center core values
  • Openness to better understand inequalities of all types
  • A desire to advocate for others
  • A willingness to volunteer, donate, and promote MW Hope Center's mission
  • Ability to attend monthly in-person meetings

Community Advisory Council

A voice for those we serve.

The mission of the Mountain West Hope Center Community Advisory Council is to ensure that the individuals, families, and communities we serve — including those experiencing or who have experienced homelessness, housing instability, incarceration, or other life challenges — have a meaningful voice in shaping the programs, services, and direction of our ministry.

The Community Advisory Council serves as a bridge between the people we serve and the leadership of the Mountain West Hope Center. Council members will make recommendations to the Hope Center Board of Directors, inform policy decisions, and help guide the development and improvement of our services — ensuring that our work remains rooted in the real needs, lived experiences, and aspirations of those at the heart of our mission.

We believe that the people closest to the challenges we address are also closest to the solutions. Your voice matters, your story matters, and your perspective has the power to shape how we serve our community across DeKalb County and beyond.

If you are passionate about making a difference and want to help ensure that the Hope Center continues to grow in impact, relevance, and compassion, we invite you to join us.
