Frequently asked

Common questions

Is MW Hope Center a faith-based organization?

Yes — our programs, including Spiritual Discipleship and Angel Tree, are rooted in a Christian, discipleship-centered approach. Contact us directly with questions about specific program requirements.

What counties do you serve?

We primarily serve families in DeKalb and Gwinnett County, GA.

How quickly will someone respond?

General messages are typically answered within 2 business days. For support requests, use the Get Help form instead.
