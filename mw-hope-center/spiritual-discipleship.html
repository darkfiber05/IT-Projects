Program 04
Spiritual Discipleship · with Prison Fellowship

Faith, Community & Angel Tree

Nurturing the spiritual growth and transformation of individuals and families through the love, grace, and teachings of Jesus Christ. We create intentional opportunities for people to encounter faith, build community, and develop a deeper relationship with God. By addressing barriers such as isolation, shame, and hopelessness, we walk alongside people at every stage of their journey, offering encouragement, accountability, and the life-changing truth that every person carries inherent worth, purpose, and the promise of a new beginning.
